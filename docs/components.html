  • 1

    Heat card

    A crime adds stars by your band (see the band card): each illegal tile kept, each theft, each haul with the illegal net. Closed water +2★ a pot. Reported +1★.
SELL with stars: roll one heat die per star and add them. Under 5: the warden takes 1 a point. 5+: BUSTED — drop the catch, no pay, the port is shut to you today.
All blanks: nerves of steel, −1★. A day you don't sell: −1★.
At 5★ a crime can't add a star: heat check on the spot (bribable). Bust = catch seized, home, launch last.
Bribe dice off one roll, down to your band's floor: each die at the price on your band card, added up.
