The idea

You captain a lobster boat on Penobscot Bay for 5 seasons. Set pots, let them soak, haul them, and sell the catch in the island ports. The ocean is shared: every lobster anyone lands is gone until the breeding stock brings some back.

The captain with the most money at the end wins. How you earn it is up to you. Fish clean, and the co-op pays you a share when the ocean is healthy. Fish dirty, keeping the lobsters the law says go back, and you land more but risk the warden. Your alignment on the light–dark track decides what is open to you; your heat, 0 to 5 stars, decides how closely the warden watches.

How a game runs

A game is 5 seasons of 4, 5, 5, 6, 6 days (26 days in all): seasons get longer as the years go on. Every day is 6 hours. Every hour, each captain who is still out takes one turn, in turn order. A turn is 2 actions.

When a season begins seasons 2–5

  1. The weather turns. Clear last season's storms. For each ground, place the number of storm tokens its row of the weather track shows (season 1: 0/0/0/0 · season 2: 0/0/1/1 · season 3: 0/1/2/1 · season 4: 0/2/3/1 · season 5: 0/2/3/1; inshore/mid/offshore/deep), using the storm die to pick which spaces. Inshore and the shelters never storm.
  2. New lobsters. Put one generic lobster on every fishing space. They pile up on spaces nobody fishes.
  3. The licence auction. Everyone writes a sealed bid on a slip, at least the season's reserve (season 2: 6, season 3: 8, season 4: 10, season 5: 12), and all bids are revealed at once. Everyone who buys pays the second-highest bid (the reserve, if fewer than two captains bid). The two highest bidders must buy; everyone else, in bid order, may buy at that price or pass. Buying steps you 1 lighter. Honest and Paragon captains must buy if they can afford it. In season 2 only some licences are for sale (2p: 1, 3p: 2, 4p: 3, 5p: 3, 6p: 4): once they are gone, the rest of the table fishes unlicensed. Without a licence you may still fish, but every haul is poaching.
  4. The bid order is this season's turn order. Highest bid first; captains who did not bid follow in seat order. You are bidding for first pick of the water as much as for the licence.

Each morning

  1. The wardens go out. Draw one patrol card, plus one for every captain on the dark side (Shady or Outlaw), up to 3. Put a warden boat on each space drawn.
  2. Turn order is the order captains berthed last night (on the first day of a season, the auction's bid order). Move the hour marker to 1.

Each hour

Going down the turn order, every captain who has not berthed takes one turn. A captain still recovering from a tow loses their turn instead. When everyone has had their turn, move the hour marker on. The day ends after hour 6, or as soon as everyone has berthed.

Your turn

You have 2 actions. Spend them on anything below, in any order, as long as you can pay each one's cost. Your turn ends when your actions are spent, or when you Pass, Berth or Bribe. Actions you don't spend are lost.

Refits that make an action free make it free every time, not once a turn. With the pot rack you can drop every pot in your hand onto the space you're on and still have both your actions to spend; with the hauling crane you can pull every ripe pot of yours on the space the same way.

Each night

  1. Everyone comes in. A captain who is still out and in a port that will have them berths there, behind everyone who berthed, in reverse of today's turn order. A captain caught at sea, or in a port closed to them, is towed to the nearest port that will have them: pay 5 money (you never go below 0), fuel goes up to at least 2, and you lose your next 4 turns. A captain busted away from the counter today launches last, and anyone busted after them launches behind them.
  2. Tomorrow's turn order is the berth order you just made.
  3. Pots soak. Advance every pot in the water one night on your pot tracker.
  4. Storms whittle. Roll the weather die for every pot on a stormed space: on 1 or less the storm parts it, and it is lost until the season changes.
  5. Heat cools. Every captain who did not sell today loses a star. Remove any "port closed" markers.
  6. The hold spoils. Every lobster in every hold loses 1 lb, to a minimum of 1 lb.
  7. Prices recover. Every market's price marker goes back to the top.

If that was the season's last day, the season ends. Otherwise move the day marker on; it is morning.

When a season ends

  1. The co-op pays its dividend. Read the ocean's health (the average of the four health tracks) off the dividend table: 80%+: 8 (Paragons 16) · 60%+: 6 (Paragons 12) · 40%+: 4 (Paragons 8) · 20%+: 2 (Paragons 4) · 0%+: 0 (Paragons 0). Every licensed captain who is Neutral or lighter is paid; Paragons read the second column.
  2. The breeding stock spawns (after seasons 1, 2, 3; never into the final season). For each ground, read its breeding stock track: 1+ breeders roll 1, 3+ breeders roll 2, 6+ breeders roll 3, 10+ breeders roll 4, 15+ breeders roll 5. Roll that many lobster dice (they average 1.5) and draw that many lobsters blind out of that ground's trap into its bag. If the trap runs out, the rest come back as generic lobsters.
  3. Everyone goes home. Every pot still in the water comes up empty and goes back to its captain's hand. Every boat returns to Rockland. Licence tokens go back to the supply. Your hold, fuel, money and refits stay with you.

After season 5 the co-op still pays its dividend, but nothing spawns and nobody goes home: the game ends. The captain with the most money wins. Ties share the win.
