Dice

Three, each doing one job.

    Lobster die

    blank / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5

    Rolled on your claim in the restock draft: how many lobsters you may return from that bag's pile. A blank wastes the claim but still locks the bag.

  • 1

    Storm die (d6)

    1–6

    Picks which ground in a tier the storm lands on. The rings are six spaces wide for exactly this reason.

  • 1

    Weather die (d10)

    1–10

    Entering a storm: a beating on 4 or less (−1 fuel). Each night, gear left in a storm parts on 1 or less — lost for the season, unless a GPS plotter finds it.

Cards & mats

Everything you would otherwise have to remember. All of it hand-computable by design — no roots, no ratios.

  • 1

    Scoring card

    Add your three tracks. Find your LOWEST. Subtract.
  lowest 40 or more → −0
  lowest 30 or more → −10
  lowest 20 or more → −30
  lowest 12 or more → −60
  lowest 6 or more → −100
  lowest below 6 → −150

    Money ÷ 5 · reputation × 2 · conservation = v-notch tokens (×2) + notches made + the shared commons bonus.
